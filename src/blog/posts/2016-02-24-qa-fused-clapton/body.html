Can we get a fused clapton wire builder on steam engine ? like core and outer wrap wire,and how many wraps of fused clapton to hit ohm’s we like . I like 316L SS ( Please ) Thank You for your hard work

You can use the Wire Wizard for this. Start out by selecting a clapton coil in the wire builder section.

For the core, choose the parallel wire. Select your 316L and your gauge, and you should be ready to jiggle the number of wraps under coil setup until you reach the resistance you want.

Be sure to check out the other options in this calculator as well. With a bit of trial and error, you should be able to use it for most types of coils.
