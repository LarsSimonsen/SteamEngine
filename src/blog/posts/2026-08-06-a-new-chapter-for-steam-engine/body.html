Steam Engine has come a long way since the original site was launched. The calculators have been rebuilt with cleaner, more maintainable code, while preserving the practical tools and familiar workflows people rely on.

The new concepts catalog, along with catalogs for materials, mods, and batteries, brings the reference data into one searchable place, with clearer details and easier links from the calculators. It provides a stronger foundation for expanding the site’s reference information over time.

The site now has a shared responsive layout, dark and light themes, improved navigation, and a restored blog. There is still more work to do, but the goal remains the same: provide useful, free tools for anyone interested in building and experimenting.

If you find any bugs or have opinions or comments about the changes, please join the discussion on the ECF forum or Reddit.

The site also includes integrated translations in English, Norwegian Bokmål, Norwegian Nynorsk, Simplified Chinese, Arabic, Spanish, French, Polish, Ukrainian, Italian, Vietnamese, German, Swedish, Danish, Icelandic, Hindi, Bengali, European Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, and Korean.
