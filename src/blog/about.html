In February 2014, I got into rebuilding. I searched for an online coil wrapping calculator, but all I could find was spreadsheets and Ohm’s law calculators. I realized the Internet had a hole in it, which I decided to fill. Thus, the Steam Engine suite of calculators was created.

The tool was initially called vape calc due to a lack of imagination. Within the first month, the name was changed to Steam Engine.

Check out the calculators here

Hand writing web pages is good for calculators and tools where you need precise control over a dynamic and responsive layout, but it gets laborious when you just want to post some thoughts, share a link or what have you.

I decided that a dedicated blog was needed, and so here it is in all its dubious glory.
